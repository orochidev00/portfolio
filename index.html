01

El Thor

Qto cast

Enel's signature move from One Piece. A storm cloud locks over your target, lightning drops, and the ground shatters into rings that copy whatever it struck.

  1. 1 · Your client

    Press Q. It reads your aim and builds the cloud, the beam and the hand glow.

  2. 2 · Server

    Verifies range, line of sight and cooldown, then deals 50 damage in 30 studs and ragdolls NPCs.

  3. 3 · Everyone

    Bolts, black-and-white flash frames, cracks and rock rings. Nothing left behind.

02
Plunge
Fto cast


A diving slam. You leap forward, drop out of the air and hit the ground hard enough to launch anyone near the landing.


  1. 1 · Your client
    Press F. Leap, dive, land. Each phase reports to the server.
  2. 2 · Server
    Checks you're really where you claim, then runs the hitbox: 20 damage plus knockback.
  3. 3 · Everyone
    Red afterimages, an FOV punch, impact frames, dust, and rocks that sink back into the ground.
